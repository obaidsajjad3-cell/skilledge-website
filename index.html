Who We Are

Practical learning for a changing banking industry

SkillEdge is a specialised corporate training and professional development company focused on banking and financial-services professionals. We combine practical banking knowledge, regulatory awareness, industry experience and emerging technology to deliver learning participants can apply in their day-to-day roles.

  • Practitioner-led. Courses are built and delivered by professionals with hands-on banking, trade, risk, compliance and operational experience.
  • Regulation-aware. Programs are developed with close attention to applicable regulatory expectations and institutional compliance needs.
  • Application-oriented. Participants leave with practical insights, tools and techniques relevant to their roles.
  • Right-sized. Formats are designed to minimise time away from business while covering the subjects that matter.
  • Future-ready. Selected programs address AI, Generative AI, RPA, automation, RegTech and digital transformation.
